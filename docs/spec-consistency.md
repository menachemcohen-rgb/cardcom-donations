# Spec consistency check (Prompt 0)

Scope: a read-only check of `CLAUDE.md` (Part A sections 1–11, Part B sections B0–B14, Part C), with precedence C > B > A. I checked four things. (1) Identifiers that are referenced but never defined: Apex classes and methods, including arity and parameter types at each call site; custom fields; the CMDT types `Cardcom_Setting__mdt`, `Donor_Link_Setting__mdt`, `Charge_Holiday__mdt` and `Cardcom_Error_Code__mdt`; the custom settings `Charge_Settings__c` and `Charge_Run_Lock__c`; platform events, labels, custom permissions, permission sets and flows. (2) Identifiers that are defined twice with different names, types, lengths, values or signatures. (3) New conflicts that the Part C Opportunity mapping (C1/C2) creates. (4) SOQL `WHERE`/`ORDER BY` on encrypted fields. Errata E1–E16, the C2 mapping itself, and the general use of `Recurring_Pledge__c` in Part B are already resolved and are not repeated here. Line numbers refer to `CLAUDE.md` as of 2026-11-02 (4,381 lines). I confirmed every finding with grep.
